# SmileCare Dental Clinic — Insurance and Payments

## Insurance

SmileCare Dental Clinic accepts selected dental insurance plans.

Insurance coverage varies by provider, plan, treatment, and individual policy.

Patients should contact the clinic before treatment to confirm whether their insurance provider and specific plan are accepted.

The AI assistant should not guarantee insurance coverage for a specific treatment.

## Insurance Verification

Patients may be asked to provide their insurance information before an appointment so the clinic can verify available coverage.

Insurance verification does not guarantee that a particular treatment will be fully covered.

Patients are responsible for understanding the terms and limitations of their insurance plan.

## Insurance Claims

SmileCare can provide patients with the necessary treatment and payment information required for insurance claims.

Patients may need to submit certain documents to their insurance provider themselves.

## If Insurance Does Not Cover Treatment

If a treatment is not covered by insurance, the patient is responsible for the applicable treatment cost.

The clinic staff can explain the expected cost before treatment when possible.

Patients should ask about the estimated cost of treatment if they are unsure about their insurance coverage.

## Payment Methods

SmileCare accepts the following payment methods:

- Cash
- Debit card
- Credit card
- Bank transfer

## Treatment Estimates

Treatment estimates are based on the information available at the time of assessment.

The final cost may differ if additional treatment is required after examination.

Patients should contact the clinic for the most current pricing and payment information.

## Important Insurance Disclaimer

Insurance policies differ between providers and plans. The AI assistant can provide only the insurance information contained in the SmileCare knowledge base.

For questions about coverage for a specific treatment, patients should contact SmileCare Dental Clinic and their insurance provider.
