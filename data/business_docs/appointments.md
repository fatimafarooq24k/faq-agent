# SmileCare Dental Clinic — Appointments and Policies

## Booking an Appointment

Patients can request an appointment by calling SmileCare Dental Clinic at:

+92 300 555 0147

Appointment requests can also be made by email:

hello@smilecare-demo.pk

When requesting an appointment, patients should provide:

- Full name
- Preferred date
- Preferred time
- Reason for the visit
- Contact number

Appointments are subject to availability.

## Walk-In Visits

Walk-in patients may be accommodated depending on clinic capacity and the urgency of the situation.

A walk-in visit does not guarantee immediate treatment.

Patients are encouraged to schedule an appointment in advance.

## New Patients

New patients should arrive approximately 10 minutes before their scheduled appointment to allow time for registration.

Patients should bring relevant medical or dental records if available.

## Rescheduling an Appointment

Patients who cannot attend their scheduled appointment should contact the clinic as soon as possible to request a new appointment time.

Rescheduling is subject to availability.

## Cancellation Policy

Patients should cancel or reschedule an appointment at least 24 hours before the scheduled appointment whenever possible.

Repeated late cancellations or missed appointments may affect future appointment scheduling.

## Late Arrival Policy

Patients arriving more than 15 minutes late may need to reschedule their appointment depending on the clinic's schedule.

The clinic will make reasonable efforts to accommodate late arrivals when possible.

## Missed Appointments

A missed appointment is an appointment that the patient does not attend and does not cancel in advance.

Patients who repeatedly miss appointments may be required to confirm future appointments in advance.

## Emergency Appointments

SmileCare accepts requests for dental emergency appointments during regular clinic hours.

Emergency appointments are subject to dentist and clinic availability.

Patients should call the clinic rather than relying on the chatbot to determine whether immediate treatment is available.

## Emergency Situations

Patients experiencing severe facial swelling that affects breathing, uncontrolled bleeding, loss of consciousness, or another life-threatening condition should seek immediate emergency medical care.

The clinic's AI assistant cannot diagnose medical emergencies or replace professional medical assessment.

## Appointment Confirmation

An appointment is considered confirmed only after the clinic has confirmed the appointment with the patient.

Submitting an appointment request does not guarantee that a specific date or time is available.
