# SmileCare Dental Clinic — Frequently Asked Questions

## What are your opening hours?

SmileCare Dental Clinic is open Monday through Friday from 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM and Saturday from 10:00 AM to 2:00 PM.

The clinic is closed on Sundays.

## Where is SmileCare Dental Clinic located?

SmileCare Dental Clinic is located at:

24 Garden Avenue  
Lahore, Punjab, Pakistan

## How can I book an appointment?

You can request an appointment by calling +92 300 555 0147 or emailing hello@smilecare-demo.pk.

Please provide your name, preferred date and time, reason for the visit, and contact number.

## Do I need an appointment?

Appointments are recommended.

Walk-in visits may be accommodated depending on clinic availability, but immediate treatment is not guaranteed.

## How much does a dental cleaning cost?

Standard teeth cleaning starts at PKR 4,000.

The final cost may vary depending on the patient's individual needs.

## How much does a filling cost?

Dental fillings start at PKR 3,500 per tooth.

The final cost depends on the type of filling and treatment required.

## How much does a root canal cost?

Root canal treatment starts at PKR 18,000 per tooth.

A dentist must examine the tooth to determine the appropriate treatment plan.

## Do you offer teeth whitening?

Yes. SmileCare offers professional teeth whitening.

Teeth whitening starts at PKR 15,000.

## Do you offer braces?

Yes. SmileCare provides orthodontic consultations for braces and clear aligners.

The cost depends on the patient's treatment plan and complexity.

## Do you treat children?

Yes. SmileCare provides general dental care for children.

Parents or guardians should provide the child's age when booking an appointment.

## Do you accept insurance?

SmileCare accepts selected dental insurance plans.

Patients should contact the clinic before treatment to confirm whether their specific insurance plan is accepted and what coverage may be available.

## What payment methods do you accept?

SmileCare accepts cash, debit cards, credit cards, and bank transfers.

## Can I cancel my appointment?

Yes. Patients should cancel or reschedule at least 24 hours before their appointment whenever possible.

Repeated late cancellations or missed appointments may affect future scheduling.

## What happens if I arrive late?

Patients arriving more than 15 minutes late may need to reschedule depending on the clinic's schedule.

## Do you offer emergency appointments?

Yes. SmileCare accepts requests for dental emergency appointments during regular clinic hours, subject to dentist and clinic availability.

Patients should call the clinic to discuss an emergency appointment.

## What should I do during a serious dental emergency?

If you have severe facial swelling affecting breathing, uncontrolled bleeding, loss of consciousness, or another life-threatening emergency, seek immediate emergency medical care.

The AI assistant cannot diagnose emergencies or replace professional medical assessment.

## Can I get the exact price of my treatment from the chatbot?

The chatbot can provide the starting prices listed in the SmileCare knowledge base.

It cannot provide a personalized final treatment estimate.

Patients should contact the clinic for current pricing and treatment estimates.

## Does the chatbot provide medical or dental diagnosis?

No.

The SmileCare AI assistant provides information from the clinic's knowledge base. It does not diagnose dental conditions or replace advice from a qualified dental professional.

## What if my question is not about SmileCare?

The AI assistant is designed to answer questions about SmileCare Dental Clinic, including its services, appointments, policies, insurance, and general clinic information.

For questions outside the clinic's knowledge base, the assistant should explain that it does not have information about that topic rather than guessing.
