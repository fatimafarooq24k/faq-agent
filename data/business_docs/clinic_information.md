## Emergency Contact Policy

Dental emergencies are handled during regular clinic hours when possible.

Patients experiencing a dental emergency should call the clinic as soon as possible so the staff can determine the appropriate next step and check for an available appointment.

If a patient is experiencing a life-threatening emergency, severe facial swelling affecting breathing, uncontrolled bleeding, or another serious medical emergency, they should seek immediate emergency medical care rather than waiting for a dental appointment.

## General Information

Appointments are recommended for all treatments.

Walk-in visits may be accommodated depending on staff availability and the urgency of the situation. A walk-in visit does not guarantee immediate treatment.

Patients should bring any relevant medical or dental records when visiting the clinic for the first time.
