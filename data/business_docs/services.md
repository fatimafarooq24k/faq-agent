# SmileCare Dental Clinic — Services

SmileCare Dental Clinic provides a range of general, preventive, restorative, and cosmetic dental services.

## General Dentistry

General dentistry services include routine dental examinations, diagnosis, preventive care, and treatment planning.

A standard dental examination includes an assessment of the patient's oral health and a discussion of recommended treatment where appropriate.

## Teeth Cleaning

Professional teeth cleaning is available for patients who need routine preventive dental care.

The standard cleaning appointment includes professional cleaning and polishing.

### Teeth Cleaning Price

Standard teeth cleaning starts at PKR 4,000.

The final cost may vary depending on the patient's individual needs.

## Dental Fillings

SmileCare provides dental fillings for teeth affected by cavities or minor tooth damage.

### Dental Filling Price

Dental fillings start at PKR 3,500 per tooth.

The final price depends on the type of filling and the treatment required.

## Root Canal Treatment

Root canal treatment is available for teeth that require treatment of the infected or inflamed dental pulp.

### Root Canal Price

Root canal treatment starts at PKR 18,000 per tooth.

The final treatment plan and cost are determined after a dental examination.

## Teeth Whitening

SmileCare offers professional teeth whitening for patients interested in improving the appearance of their smile.

### Teeth Whitening Price

Professional teeth whitening starts at PKR 15,000.

A consultation may be recommended before whitening treatment.

## Braces and Clear Aligners

SmileCare provides orthodontic consultations for patients interested in braces or clear aligners.

Treatment options depend on the patient's dental and orthodontic assessment.

### Orthodontic Treatment

The cost of braces or clear aligners varies significantly depending on the treatment plan, duration, and complexity.

Patients should schedule an orthodontic consultation to receive an appropriate treatment estimate.

## Dental Crowns

Dental crowns may be recommended when a tooth requires additional protection or restoration.

### Dental Crown Price

Dental crowns start at PKR 20,000 per tooth.

The final price depends on the type of crown and the patient's treatment requirements.

## Dental X-Rays

Dental X-rays may be recommended as part of diagnosis or treatment planning.

### Dental X-Ray Price

Dental X-rays start at PKR 1,500.

The type and number of X-rays required depend on the patient's dental needs.

## Children's Dentistry

SmileCare provides general dental care for children, including routine examinations and preventive dental care.

Parents or guardians should mention the child's age when booking an appointment.

## Service Availability

Not every treatment is appropriate for every patient.

The dentist will evaluate the patient's condition and recommend an appropriate treatment plan.

Prices listed in this document are starting prices and may change. Patients should contact SmileCare Dental Clinic for the latest pricing information.
